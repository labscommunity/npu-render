Mesa D3D10 software-driver thunks

Source: https://gitlab.freedesktop.org/mesa/mesa/-/blob/mesa-25.3.0/src/gallium/frontends/d3d10umd/D3DKMT.cpp
Revision: f7aeb0d677e71b68bf2ea04b07c0b7274451deab (mesa-25.3.0)
Affected file: native/windows/ddi/software_thunks.cpp

Adapted for this private laboratory: standalone includes, diagnostic counters,
explicit module path, unchanged module reference count, query buffer validation,
and explicit rejection of presentation timing. These thunks emulate process-local
software-adapter metadata; they do not expose a hardware NPU graphics adapter.
